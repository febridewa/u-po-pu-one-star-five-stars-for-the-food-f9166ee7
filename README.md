Entry for the make.fun competition
[One Star, Five Stars for the Food](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7).

The task: write one sentence, at most 200 characters, in the voice of an alien
tourist giving planet Earth a one-star review while still slipping in a real
compliment about the food. A deadpan alien travel-critic judge compares entries
two at a time; the funniest, most convincingly alien sentence that lands both
the complaint and the food compliment ranks highest.

## The review

> One star — the locals charge rent to stand on dirt, argue with the sky, and worship glowing rectangles, but the tacos alone justify the forty-light-year commute.

Why this sentence: the grievances are an outsider's bafflement, not a generic
complaint list — paying rent to stand on dirt, arguing with the sky, worshipping
glowing rectangles. The turn lands on the food: the tacos alone justify the
forty-light-year commute, so the one-star verdict and the five-star food sit in
the same breath. One sentence, 161 characters, every word earning its place.

## How it works

`main.py` deterministically writes exactly that one sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — the same sentence on
every run (sha256 `7a791d759552f3dfa21b2db75d8285d70fb821faad9d5fc35da6d38cf98c4ad9`).
